## ⭐ Features that will make CoDO stand out

Rather than competing directly with existing platforms, focus on features that combine opportunities with collaboration:

1. **Official organisation events** with analytics.
2. **One-click "Looking for Team"** on every event.
3. **Project recruitment** (find contributors for ongoing projects).
4. **AI teammate recommendations** based on skills and availability.
5. **Verified student portfolios** showcasing projects, certifications and achievements.
6. **Cross-college networking**, allowing students to discover collaborators beyond their own campus.
7. **Skill-based search** to find people by technology, experience and interests.
8. **Integrated team workspaces** with chat, shared files and task tracking for each team.

These are the kinds of features that give CoDO its own identity as a **student collaboration platform**, rather than just another event listing or professional networking site.
