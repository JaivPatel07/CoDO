Frequently Asked Questions

Is CoDO free for students? add remove
Yes, CoDO is completely free for students. You just need to verify your institutional email address to gain full access to the platform's features.
How can my club join the platform? add remove
Student organizations can apply for a "Verified Org" status. Once verified, you can list events, manage members, and recruit new talent directly from the platform.
Can I use CoDO if I'm not a tech major? add remove
Absolutely! CoDO is for all students—designers, business majors, writers, scientists, and more. Collaborative projects require diverse skillsets.
